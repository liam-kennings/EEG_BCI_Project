# V7 Poster Layout Options

All three mockups use a 48 x 36 inch landscape canvas, a low-ink off-white
background, dark high-contrast text, and a restrained blue/teal/gold/coral
palette. Use the PDF or SVG export for final printing; the PNG files are
intended primarily for review and PowerPoint placement.

## Recommended: Layout A Story Flow

Best for a broad poster audience. This version follows the same sequence used
to explain the project aloud, removes the separate dataset summary because
those data already appear in the pipeline, and uses black body text:

1. Why weak motor-intent detection matters and what problem must be addressed.
2. The general approach followed by the complete V7 pipeline.
3. Experimental strengths and limitations alongside published context.
4. Experimental protocol, new dataset creation, further training, and device
   or multimodal-sensor testing.

The process-only pipeline and result panels have separate roles, avoiding
repeated performance numbers. The recommended final editing base is provided
as `layout_a_story_flow_final_editing_base.pdf`, `.svg`, and `.png`.

## Layout B - Evidence First

Best for technical judging or a methods-focused conference. It gives the
largest area to quantitative results and places interpretation, published
context, and the next validation decision alongside the evidence. The method
is summarized as a compact visual strip rather than a second text-heavy panel.

## Layout C - Translational Journey

Best for clinical, rehabilitation, or PhD-proposal framing. It emphasizes the
transition from an unmet clinical need to a population model, rapid patient
adaptation, a binary safety gate, and prospective device integration. It is
the clearest option when the research trajectory is as important as the
current benchmark result.

## Print Guidance

- Print from PDF or SVG whenever the print service accepts them.
- Keep the final page at 48 x 36 inches and avoid enlarging raster exports.
- Preserve the off-white background; it reduces glare and avoids large areas
  of saturated ink.
- Keep body text at 28 pt or larger at final size, section headers at 36 pt or
  larger, and the title at approximately 66 pt or larger.
- Ask the printer to preserve embedded profiles and perform a CMYK soft proof;
  the palette was chosen for separation under common CMYK conversion.
- Print a letter-size proof first to check hierarchy, then inspect a full-size
  proof from approximately 4-6 feet away.
