## Acknowledgments

This project used publicly available EEG and EEG-EMG data from PhysioNet
EEGMMIDB, OpenBMI, the Liu acute-stroke motor-imagery dataset, the MRCP EEG-EMG
hand-gesture dataset, WAY-EEG-GAL, and HS-Stroke. We thank the original
investigators and participants who made these resources available. Use of
WAY-EEG-GAL is additionally acknowledged as part of the WAY project
(FP7-ICT-288551).

*Dataset-use note: The reported V7 benchmark was developed and evaluated with
EEGMMIDB, OpenBMI, and Liu acute-stroke data. MRCP EEG-EMG and WAY-EEG-GAL were
used in supporting transfer experiments, while HS-Stroke was evaluated as a
separate locked chronic-stroke cohort.*
